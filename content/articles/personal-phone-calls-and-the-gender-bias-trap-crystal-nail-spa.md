*Customer Service Bulletin — Professional Conduct & Men's Grooming Feature*

A nail salon that brands itself as a "Spa" — a place for recovery and relaxation — instantly undermines itself when its staff behave as if they are lounging in their own living rooms. A scathing review from a male client regarding Crystal Nail Spa exposes a disastrous picture of workplace etiquette: a technician engrossed in a personal phone call, blatant corner-cutting driven by gender bias, and staff members openly sleeping on the salon floor.

## The Endless Phone Call and Ultimate Disregard

The male customer came in for a pedicure. However, throughout the entire service, the female technician did not speak a single word to him. Instead, she was on her phone having a personal conversation the entire time. "Which is fine if your work is good," the customer wearily noted. Unfortunately, the reality was exactly the opposite.

## Corner-Cutting Fueled by "He's a Man, He Doesn't Know Any Better"

Taking advantage of the client's gender, the technician operated under the deeply flawed assumption that he was completely "ignorant to the process," prompting her to blatantly skip essential steps.

The result was an absolute butchering of his toes: the nails were cut unevenly, not a single nail was smoothed out, no lubricant or lotion treatment was applied, and she skipped over all the basics of a standard pedicure. The technician severely underestimated her client; while not a pro, he had been to salons enough times to know exactly how the process should work — and when he was being blatantly scammed, or as he put it, when someone was trying to "get over on you."

## "Trash" Culture and Toxic Vibes

The customer's experience hit rock bottom when he looked around the shop only to see other staff members literally sleeping throughout the salon. This lethargic, lazy atmosphere led him to describe the customer service as "trash," giving off incredibly "bad vibes." The client was so disgusted that he issued a public warning to all men in the area seeking self-care to stay far away from this establishment.

## Media Expert's Perspective

This nail salon is violating the absolute bare minimum standards of the hospitality industry. Being on a personal phone call while physically working on a client's hands or feet is the ultimate display of disrespect. Furthermore, cutting corners simply because the client is male is a fatal error in an era where the "Men's Grooming" market is booming — alienating a male client today means forfeiting one of the fastest-growing segments in the entire beauty industry. Finally, the sight of staff sleeping in plain view of paying customers turns a supposedly professional "Spa" into a rundown waiting room, completely destroying the brand's image. The fix costs nothing but discipline: phones away during service, the same full protocol for every client regardless of gender, and a floor that always looks staffed and awake.
