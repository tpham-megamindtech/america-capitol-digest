*Customer Service Bulletin — Medical Safety & Crisis Management Feature*

A routine salon visit should never end with months of pain, permanent physical damage, and unanswered requests for help. According to the customer, what began as a standard pedicure ultimately resulted in nearly a year of suffering, medical treatment exceeding $700, and a lasting loss of trust.

## The Arrogant Owner and the Brazen Lie

The customer alleged that owner Jessica acted as though she was unwilling to perform the requested pedicure. Instead, she claimed to have a medical condition and reassigned the service to another technician named Victor.

During the appointment, however, the customer said Jessica gave herself a pedicure while eating snacks and watching television. From the client's perspective, this made the earlier explanation appear dishonest and immediately damaged confidence in the salon.

## Physical Devastation and the $700 Medical Bill

According to the review, Victor filed the customer's toenails so aggressively that the surrounding skin began peeling. Believing the discomfort would pass, the client completed the appointment and even left a tip.

Instead, the pain became worse over the following months. She eventually visited a podiatrist and reportedly underwent surgical toenail removal. Medical expenses exceeded $700, recovery lasted close to a year, and the nail allegedly regrew black, uneven, and permanently deformed. The customer stated she no longer felt comfortable wearing open-toed shoes.

## Blocking Numbers, Evading, and Dodging Accountability

Rather than receiving assistance, the customer claimed the salon ignored repeated phone calls and three emails, even after she provided an itemized medical bill. She further alleged that her phone number was eventually blocked, no refund was offered, and no compensation for the medical treatment was discussed.

The communication breakdown transformed a service complaint into a significant reputation issue.

## Media Expert's Perspective

Whether every allegation can be independently verified or not, this case illustrates the importance of immediate, compassionate crisis management. Any report involving physical injury should trigger documentation, management review, insurance notification, and clear communication with the customer.

Technical mistakes may happen in any service business. What often determines long-term reputation is not the original mistake, but how responsibly and professionally the business responds afterward.
