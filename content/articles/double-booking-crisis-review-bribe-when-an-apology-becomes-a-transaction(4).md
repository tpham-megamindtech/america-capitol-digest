*Customer Service Bulletin — Front Desk Management & Crisis Ethics Feature*

A scheduling mistake is frustrating but recoverable. As this case involving **La Dolce Nail Spa Humble** illustrates, repeated appointment errors and a poorly handled follow-up can quickly undo customer trust that might otherwise have been preserved.

## Front Desk Flaws and the Waste of Time

According to the customer, the salon generally provided friendly service, but the front desk repeatedly failed to manage appointments correctly. This was the second time she had been double-booked with her preferred technician, Nina.

She arrived for her scheduled 9:00 AM appointment only to discover that another client was already occupying the same technician's time. Staff informed her she would need to wait approximately three and a half hours.

The customer explained she had recently left a nail salon she had visited for seven years because this location was much more convenient. Unfortunately, the repeated scheduling mistakes quickly erased that positive first impression.

## When an Apology Became a Transaction

After the customer left, the salon contacted her and offered a complimentary full set of nails.

However, according to the review, the complimentary service came with an expectation that she update or change her online review.

Rather than rebuilding trust, the request left the customer feeling that protecting the salon's online reputation mattered more than acknowledging the inconvenience she had experienced.

Although she accepted the complimentary service, she later updated her review to include the interaction and stated she would not return.

## Media Expert's Perspective

Double-booking is an operational problem that can usually be resolved through prompt communication, sincere apologies, and fair recovery efforts. Attempting to tie compensation to changes in a customer's public review, however, risks undermining credibility and customer confidence. A transparent apology and an unconditional goodwill gesture would likely have preserved the relationship and demonstrated genuine accountability.
