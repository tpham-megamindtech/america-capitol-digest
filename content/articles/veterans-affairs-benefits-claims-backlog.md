The Department of Veterans Affairs says it has made steady progress reducing its benefits claims backlog, crediting a mix of new hires and automation that speeds routine steps in the review process.

The improvement follows a surge in claims after eligibility expansions brought more veterans into the system. Officials say the added volume initially lengthened waits before staffing and technology investments began to pay off.

## Where delays remain

Straightforward claims now move faster, but complex cases involving multiple conditions or extensive medical records still take time. Veterans' advocates urge continued investment, noting that a decision delayed can mean real hardship.

Automation handles document sorting and eligibility checks, freeing human reviewers to focus on judgment calls. The department stresses that people, not algorithms, still make the final determinations on awards.

The backlog has been a recurring subject of oversight for years, and lawmakers say they will keep watching the numbers. For veterans awaiting decisions from Texas to Maine, the trend matters more than the rhetoric.
