Merit, the minimalist makeup brand that built a following on a deliberately small edit of products, has secured a new minority investment and expanded beyond cosmetics into skincare and fragrance, a notable step for one of the buzzier names in modern beauty.

The investment, from a beauty- and wellness-focused firm, gives the brand fresh backing as it broadens its catalog. Founded earlier this decade, Merit made its name by rejecting the maximalism of much of the industry, offering a curated range designed to simplify routines rather than expand them.

## From makeup to a fuller routine

The move into skincare and fragrance reflects a common path for successful beauty brands: once a label earns customer trust in one category, extending into adjacent ones can deepen loyalty and lift spending per customer. The challenge is doing so without diluting the minimalist identity that drew shoppers in the first place.

## A category in motion

The deal is part of a busy stretch of investment and expansion across the beauty industry, where established players and newer entrants alike are jockeying for shelf space and attention. Minimalist, "less but better" positioning has resonated with shoppers wary of complicated regimens and eager for products that do a few things well.

For Merit, the test now is execution — carrying its restrained aesthetic and point of view into new categories where the competition is fierce and customer expectations, shaped by the broader skincare boom, run high.
