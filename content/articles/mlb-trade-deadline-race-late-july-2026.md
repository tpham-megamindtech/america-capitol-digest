With Major League Baseball's trade deadline days away, contending teams spent the final stretch of July maneuvering around a market headlined by front-line pitching, setting the stage for a frantic finish to the sport's midsummer reshuffle.

The biggest name generating rumors was Tigers ace Tarik Skubal, whose potential availability loomed over the market and drew interest from clubs chasing October. Several other established pitchers and hitters were also the subject of speculation as buyers and sellers sized one another up.

## Buyers and sellers

The deadline forces every team to choose a lane. Contenders weigh how much of their farm system to spend chasing a title, while clubs out of the race decide whether to cash in veterans for prospects. Those calculations intensify in the final days, when leverage shifts and deals can come together quickly.

## Pitching at a premium

As is often the case, pitching commanded the highest prices. Front-line starters are the currency of postseason baseball, and the teams positioning themselves for deep runs were willing to pay steeply to add arms. The market's biggest dominoes hinged on whether the marquee names would actually move.

With standings tightly bunched in several divisions, the stakes were high: a well-timed acquisition can tilt a race, while standing pat can leave a contender exposed. As the clock ticked toward the deadline, front offices across the league braced for a hectic close to the trading season.
