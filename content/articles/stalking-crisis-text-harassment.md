# The "Stalking" Crisis and Text Harassment: When an Owner's Ego Turns Praise into a Nightmare

**Customer Service Bulletin – Customer Privacy & Professional Boundaries Feature**

According to one customer review involving **First Class Nails & Spa**, an appointment that initially earned positive feedback later became a source of significant concern after interactions that the customer believed crossed personal boundaries. She said the experience ultimately changed her opinion of the business despite being satisfied with much of the service itself.

## A Mostly Positive Visit

The customer praised the salon's pedicure, massage, and the use of well-known professional nail products. She also mentioned a few concerns, including a fee for a disposable nail kit, the absence of online booking, and payment practices that she believed could have been explained more clearly.

Despite those issues, she said she initially posted a **4-star review** because she felt the overall experience was largely positive.

## Concerns After the Review

According to the customer, shortly after posting the review from her car, a man she believed was associated with the salon approached her vehicle in the parking lot to discuss comments she had made about a credit card fee.

She said the encounter left her uncomfortable because she had not expected to be approached after leaving the business.

## Follow-Up Messages

The customer further alleged that after she drove away, she received text messages from the same individual using the phone number she had previously provided for her appointment.

According to her review, she asked that the messages stop because she believed the communication had become inappropriate. She said this experience ultimately led her to lower her rating and decide not to return.

## Editorial Perspective

Customer feedback, including constructive criticism, can provide valuable opportunities for businesses to improve. When responding to reviews, respectful communication and appropriate professional boundaries are essential to maintaining customer confidence.

Likewise, businesses that collect customer contact information should use it in ways that are consistent with customer expectations and applicable privacy practices.

## Key Takeaways

- Constructive feedback can be an opportunity for improvement.
- Professional boundaries help customers feel respected.
- Customer contact information should be handled responsibly.
- Respectful communication supports long-term trust.
- Effective service recovery can strengthen business reputation.

## Final Thoughts

The experience described in this customer review illustrates how post-service communication can significantly influence customer perception. For beauty businesses, professionalism, respect for customer privacy, and thoughtful responses to feedback remain essential to building lasting relationships.
