Wellness tourism is booming across the United States, as travelers increasingly plan getaways around rest and restoration rather than mere sightseeing. Destination spas, retreats, and wellness-focused resorts are thriving.

The trend reflects a broader cultural investment in wellbeing, and a recognition that vacations can leave people more depleted than restored. Wellness travel promises the opposite: to return home genuinely recharged.

## What travelers seek

Offerings range widely, from luxury spa resorts to rugged retreats built around hiking, yoga, or digital detox. The common thread is intentionality, structuring a trip around movement, healthy food, sleep, and time away from screens.

The category spans budgets. While marquee retreats command premium prices, the underlying idea, traveling to rest and reset, has spread to more accessible getaways and even day trips to local spas and hot springs.

## Substance and skepticism

Experts encourage travelers to enjoy the genuine benefits, relaxation, movement, and a break from routine, while keeping a critical eye on wellness claims that promise more than a getaway can deliver. The best of these trips, they say, model habits worth bringing home.
