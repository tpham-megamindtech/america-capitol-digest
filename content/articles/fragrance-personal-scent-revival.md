Fragrance is enjoying a vibrant revival, embraced by a new generation as a form of everyday self-expression. Interest in perfume, once associated with special occasions, has become part of daily routine for many.

Social media has fueled the trend, turning scent, an inherently unshareable sense, into content through reviews, recommendations, and the theater of unboxing. Niche and artisanal houses have found audiences far beyond their traditional reach.

## Layering and personalization

A hallmark of the revival is personalization. Enthusiasts layer scents to create signatures uniquely their own, mixing notes and building wardrobes of fragrances for different moods and seasons rather than settling on a single bottle.

The category spans price points, from accessible body mists to costly extraits, and the culture prizes knowledge and discovery over mere luxury. Learning the vocabulary of notes and families has become part of the appeal.

## Scent and memory

Part of fragrance's enduring pull is its tie to memory and emotion, a connection rooted in how the sense of smell is wired to the brain. In an increasingly digital world, experts suggest, a sense that resists screens holds a particular, tactile charm.
