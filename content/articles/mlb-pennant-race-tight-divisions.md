As the baseball season reaches its midsummer stretch, several divisions remain tightly bunched, setting up a tense race toward the postseason. The refusal of contenders to separate has made for compelling standings and high-stakes series.

The long grind of a baseball season tends to sort teams eventually, but this year the sorting has been slow. Clubs that looked like clear favorites have been pushed by unexpected challengers, keeping the races honest deep into the summer.

## The trade deadline looms

With the trade deadline approaching, contending teams face familiar decisions about whether to add for a playoff push, and at what cost to their farm systems. The moves made in the coming weeks could tip balanced divisions one way or another.

Pitching depth, always the currency of a long season, has proven decisive. Teams that stayed healthy on the mound have hung in the races, while those hit by injuries have scrambled to patch rotations and bullpens.

## A grueling finish

The beauty of a tight race is that every game carries weight, and the daily rhythm of baseball rewards teams that stay steady over the marathon. As the stretch run approaches, several clubs know that the margin between October baseball and an early winter is razor thin.
