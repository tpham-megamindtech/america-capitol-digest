# The "Closing Time" Crisis and the Online War of Words: When the "OPEN" Sign Becomes a Trap

**Customer Service Bulletin – Refusal Skills & Social Media Etiquette Feature**

According to a customer review involving **Nails 79 in Griffin**, a visit near closing time led to confusion over service availability and later developed into a public disagreement online. The reviewer said the experience ultimately changed her opinion of the business.

## Confusion at Closing Time

The customer stated that she arrived at approximately **6:00 PM** while the salon's **OPEN** sign was still illuminated.

According to her account, she was informed that no additional customers would be accepted. She believed the explanation was brief and unclear, leaving her uncertain why the salon appeared open but was no longer taking appointments.

The reviewer suggested that updating signage or providing a clearer explanation at the entrance could have reduced confusion.

## The Online Exchange

After the visit, the customer posted a negative online review describing her experience.

According to the review, representatives from the salon later responded publicly, and the discussion continued online. The customer believed a simple explanation at the time of her visit would have been more helpful than debating the issue afterward.

She said the public exchange reinforced her decision not to return.

## Editorial Perspective

Turning away walk-in customers near closing time is sometimes unavoidable, particularly when existing appointments are still being completed. However, clear communication and visible closing procedures can help customers better understand the situation.

Likewise, responding to online reviews with professionalism, empathy, and factual explanations often encourages constructive dialogue while protecting a business's reputation.

## Key Takeaways

- Clearly communicate when walk-in services are no longer available.
- Keep signage consistent with operating procedures.
- Respond to online reviews respectfully and professionally.
- Calm communication can prevent misunderstandings from escalating.
- Positive service recovery helps build long-term customer trust.

## Final Thoughts

The experience described in this customer review illustrates how communication before and after a service request can influence customer perception. For beauty businesses, transparent expectations, courteous interactions, and thoughtful responses to feedback remain essential for maintaining lasting customer relationships.
