*Customer Service Bulletin — Front Desk Management & Crisis Ethics Feature*

As this case involving **La Dolce Nail Spa Humble** illustrates, even a simple scheduling mistake can become a lasting reputation issue when recovery efforts are handled poorly.

## Front Desk Flaws and the Waste of Time

According to the customer, this was the second time she had been double-booked with her preferred technician, Nina. She arrived for her scheduled 9:00 AM appointment only to learn another client was already being served, resulting in an estimated three-and-a-half-hour wait.

She explained that she had recently switched from a salon she had visited for seven years because this location was more convenient. The repeated scheduling problems quickly undermined that decision.

## When an Apology Became a Transaction

After she left, the salon contacted her and offered a complimentary full set of nails. However, the customer alleged the offer was tied to changing her online review.

Rather than rebuilding trust, the request reinforced her belief that protecting ratings mattered more than addressing the inconvenience she had experienced. Although she accepted the complimentary service, she updated her review to describe the interaction and decided not to return.

## Media Expert's Perspective

Double-booking is an operational issue that can often be resolved through prompt communication, sincere apologies, and fair recovery. Linking compensation to a customer's public review, however, risks damaging credibility and trust. An unconditional apology and transparent resolution would have been far more effective in preserving a potentially long-term customer relationship.
