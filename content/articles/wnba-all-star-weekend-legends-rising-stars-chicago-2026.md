*Sports News — WNBA All-Star Weekend Feature*

The WNBA is adding another highlight to its 2026 All-Star Weekend in Chicago with the return of the Shooting Stars competition, an event that blends the league's history with its next generation of talent. By pairing Hall of Fame players, current WNBA stars, and top high school prospects on the same teams, the showcase aims to celebrate the league's continued growth while giving fans a unique All-Star experience.

The announcement comes as interest in women's basketball continues to rise across the United States, with the league expanding its audience, attracting new partners, and investing in events that connect multiple generations of players.

## Hall of Famers Return to the Spotlight

Among the biggest names participating are Hall of Famers Lauren Jackson and Elena Delle Donne, joined by WNBA champions Rebekkah Brunson and Deanna "Tweety" Nolan. Each legend will captain a team featuring an active WNBA player alongside one of the country's top high school athletes.

The format is designed to showcase both basketball skill and mentorship, allowing young players to compete alongside athletes who helped shape the league while giving fans another opportunity to see legendary stars back on the court.

## Bridging the League's Past and Future

Current WNBA players will team up with elite prospects from Nike's EYBL circuit, creating a competition that extends beyond entertainment. The event reflects the league's broader effort to strengthen its player development pipeline and inspire the next generation of athletes.

Rather than focusing solely on established stars, the Shooting Stars showcase highlights how women's basketball continues to evolve through emerging talent, reinforcing the connection between youth development and the professional game.

## Growing Momentum for Women's Basketball

The showcase arrives during a period of sustained momentum for the WNBA, with growing attendance, expanding media coverage, and increasing commercial interest helping elevate the league's profile. All-Star Weekend has become more than a celebration of the season's top performers—it is also an opportunity to demonstrate the sport's expanding reach and cultural influence.

By combining legends, current stars, and future standouts in a single event, the WNBA continues to position All-Star Weekend as both a celebration of its history and a glimpse of where the league is headed.

## Sports Editor's Perspective

The return of the Shooting Stars competition reflects a broader trend across American sports: fans increasingly value experiences that tell a larger story rather than simply showcasing competition. Bringing together Hall of Famers, active professionals, and elite young athletes creates a narrative that spans generations while reinforcing the WNBA's long-term investment in talent development. As the league continues to grow, events like this help strengthen its identity—not only as a professional competition, but as a platform connecting the past, present, and future of women's basketball.
