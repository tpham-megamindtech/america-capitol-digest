The ingredients and thoughtful formulations once reserved for the face are migrating below the neck, in a trend the industry calls the skinification of body care. Serums, exfoliating treatments, and active ingredients now populate the body aisle.

For years, body care lagged behind facial skincare, dominated by basic lotions and washes. The new wave applies the same logic that transformed the face: targeted ingredients, elevated textures, and routines built around specific concerns.

## Actives for the body

Body products now feature familiar names from facial care, such as retinol for texture, alpha hydroxy acids for smoothness, and niacinamide for tone. Formats have grown more sophisticated, from lightweight serums to rich barrier creams.

The appeal is straightforward: the skin on the body faces many of the same challenges as the face, from dryness to uneven texture, and can benefit from more than an afterthought. Consumers accustomed to elevated facial routines want the same below the neck.

## Keeping it grounded

As with any trend, experts urge perspective. The basics, gentle cleansing, moisturizing, and sun protection on exposed skin, still matter most. But for those inclined to do more, body care has finally become a category worth paying attention to.
