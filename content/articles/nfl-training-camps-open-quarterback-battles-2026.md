The NFL offseason is over. Training camps have opened across the league, with rookies reporting first in mid-July and veterans on all 32 teams following through the end of the month, kicking off the long ramp toward the regular season.

As is often the case in late July, the loudest storylines center on quarterbacks. Several teams head into camp with unsettled situations under center, and the competitions — some between veterans, others involving younger players pushing for the job — will shape expectations for the season ahead.

## Contracts and holdouts

Beyond the quarterback battles, contract disputes are a familiar summer subplot. A handful of players seeking new deals have become camp storylines in their own right, with the usual questions about who will practice, who will hold out, and how the standoffs will resolve before the games count.

## Building toward September

Training camp is where rosters take shape, injuries are managed, and depth is tested — the unglamorous work that separates contenders from pretenders once the season begins. Coaches often note that the teams built to withstand injuries, rather than those with the flashiest stars, tend to be the ones quietly winning the summer.

With camps now in full swing, the preseason will follow, offering the first live looks at rookies and position battles. For fans, the return of camp is the surest sign that football is nearly back, and that the questions of July will start getting answered on the field.
