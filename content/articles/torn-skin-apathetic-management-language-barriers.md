A customer reviewing a recent visit to **Milano Nail Spa Wynnewood Village** described an experience that she said turned a relaxing self-care appointment into a painful ordeal. According to her account, a combination of overly aggressive technique, communication difficulties, and an unsatisfactory follow-up response from management left her deeply disappointed.

## A Pedicure That Resulted in Pain

The customer said she received a pedicure from a technician identified as **Lena**.

According to the review, the technician scrubbed her feet so aggressively that the skin was rubbed open in two areas, leaving raw spots and painful blisters.

The customer said she repeatedly attempted to ask the technician to be gentler. However, she believed a language barrier prevented her concerns from being understood, and the service continued without adjustment.

## A Weekend Trip Impacted

The customer explained that the appointment took place shortly before a planned weekend trip to Austin with friends.

She said the injuries made it painful to wear heels or sandals, and even routine activities such as showering caused discomfort because soap and water irritated the affected areas.

According to her review, what was intended to be a relaxing preparation for the trip instead became a source of ongoing pain throughout the weekend.

## Concerns About the Follow-Up

After returning home, the customer said she contacted the salon several times and provided photographs showing the condition of her feet.

According to her account, she was informed that the salon maintained a non-refundable policy. She also stated that management promised to return her call but never followed up.

The customer said the lack of communication ultimately left her feeling that her concerns had not been taken seriously.

## Editorial Perspective

Beauty and wellness services should prioritize customer safety above all else.

When a customer reports a physical injury, prompt communication, empathy, and careful documentation can play an important role in maintaining trust, regardless of the final outcome. Even when businesses have established refund policies, customers often expect meaningful follow-up when concerns involve potential injury rather than dissatisfaction alone.

Communication is equally important during services. If a technician and customer struggle to understand one another, pausing to clarify comfort levels may help reduce the risk of misunderstandings and improve the overall experience.

## Key Takeaways

- Customer comfort should be monitored throughout every service.
- Clear communication helps technicians respond to discomfort promptly.
- Reported injuries deserve timely follow-up and professional attention.
- Consistent customer communication strengthens trust during complaint resolution.
- Strong crisis management can help preserve long-term business reputation.

## Final Thoughts

The experience described in this review illustrates how technical execution, communication, and post-service support can all shape a customer's lasting impression. For beauty businesses, responding promptly and professionally to safety-related concerns remains an essential part of delivering quality customer care.
