Nail care is trending toward a balance of style and health, as more Americans prioritize the condition of their nails alongside how they look. Durable, lower-damage options are reshaping the manicure.

The shift mirrors the broader wellness turn in beauty. After years in which some popular treatments left nails brittle or thinned, consumers are seeking options that deliver longevity without as much damage to the natural nail.

## Longevity meets care

Long-wear formulas that resist chipping have grown more sophisticated, and salons increasingly offer treatments framed around nail health, including strengthening bases and gentler removal processes. Breaks between treatments to let nails recover have become common advice.

Simplicity is also in vogue. Neutral tones, minimalist designs, and clean, well-kept nails have gained ground over elaborate, high-maintenance styles, in keeping with the natural aesthetic sweeping beauty more broadly.

## Sensible habits

Experts emphasize fundamentals that no product replaces: keeping nails and cuticles moisturized, avoiding using nails as tools, and giving them periodic rest. Healthy nails, like healthy skin and hair, reward consistent, gentle care over dramatic intervention.
