American tennis is enjoying a resurgence on the professional tour, as a crop of rising players restores the country's presence near the top of the game after years in which its stars were largely absent from the sport's biggest stages.

The revival spans both the men's and women's games, with young Americans breaking into the upper ranks and making deep runs at major tournaments. Their progress has rekindled domestic interest in a sport that had drifted from the national spotlight.

## Building through the ranks

The resurgence reflects patient development through junior and collegiate pathways, as well as investment in coaching and infrastructure. Producing tour-level talent is a long process, and the current generation is the fruit of years of groundwork.

The players themselves bring varied styles and personalities, from big servers to relentless baseliners, giving American fans a range of talents to follow. Their rivalries with the sport's international stars have made for compelling viewing.

## Sustaining success

The challenge in tennis, an individual sport with a brutal global grind, is turning promising breakthroughs into sustained success at the highest level. For now, the depth of emerging American talent offers reason for optimism that the resurgence is more than a fleeting moment.
